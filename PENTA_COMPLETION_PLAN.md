## Goal and acceptance contract

Every supported, permitted task must be reachable by conversation. Target 90% of
routine operational work through AI, with Manual always usable. Coverage 100% and
usage 90% are different measures. Neither target is accepted today. Required
approvals, attachments and secure entry can be part of the conversational journey;
never call a model plan or a queued request a completed domain operation.

One task-ledger row per role/business outcome: existing Manual route/API/service,
current tool, request/result schemas, permissions/module/tenant policy, required
and optional fields, duplicate/concurrency rules, confirmation/approval, audit,
idempotent receipt/recovery, frontend states, model/runtime pin, held-out verdict,
Manual equivalent and evidence. Reuse QA inventories, host implementation status
and existing native tools; transport endpoints are not the outcome denominator.
No missing field may be silently invented, and optional fields remain optional.

Security/credential administration restrictions stay explicit. PENTA must not
change roles/security configuration, authorize itself or execute arbitrary SQL/
code. Conversation may direct the owner to the governed secure control. Such
exceptions must be shown in coverage; they cannot be hidden to claim 100% autonomy.
