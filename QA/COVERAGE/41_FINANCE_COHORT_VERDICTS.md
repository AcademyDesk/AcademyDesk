## Cross-row reconciliation and distinct new finding

The source comparison confirms the earlier P0 balance split: Invoice.List counts non-Voided payments; Payments.Create counts **only Completed**, compares against gross `TotalAmount`, and sets invoice status from that sum; FinanceAdjustments.Decide counts Completed or Reconciled after increasing `AdjustedAmount`. These are three different paid/balance computations and require one independent decimal oracle across operations. Existing BUG-DATA-0001/0002/0010 capture the mismatches. Do not close any of them because a row has a complete test specification.

Invoices.UpdateStatus has an additional independent bypass: an authorized direct caller can set Paid for a zero-payment invoice, or send lowercase `paid`, which the allowlist accepts and persists. It consults no payment or adjustment state. This is new P1 **BUG-DATA-0055 / FINANCE-INVOICE-STATUS-001**, not a rewording of BUG-DATA-0010 (which concerns changing a *payment* status without updating its invoice). Real HTTP/SQL confirmation and the policy for administrative overrides remain pending.

Effective G03 remains as Batch 02 specifies: active same-tenant users, active academy and required enabled module, then Owner/AcademyAdmin or finance.manage; flagged PlatformOwner bypasses that academy filter, but a role name alone does not. Invoices/Payments map Finance, Adjustments maps FinanceControls. UI lookup dependencies can make FinanceUser pages unusable even when their finance actions pass; BUG-FUNC-0006 covers that. Full-pipeline role, module, wrong-tenant and no-write evidence is NOT RUN.
