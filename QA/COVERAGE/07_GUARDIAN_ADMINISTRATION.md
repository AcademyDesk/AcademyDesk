### Link flags and lifecycle (API-only on these pages)

LinkGuardianRequest includes AllowParentPortalAccess(default false), AllowAcademicProgress/AllowFinance/AllowDocuments/AllowLeave(default true), absent from the link form. Minor = DOB present and DOB+18 years later than UTC today. Link sets portal access to minor OR requested flag, then each subpermission to portalAccess AND flag; grant timestamp set only when enabled. Thus the UI's omitted flags yield all permissions enabled for minors and no portal access for adults/unknown DOB. Contact association does not provision an Identity account. Inactive student/guardian records pass existence checks; selectors also include inactive guardians without an inactive label. Test intended eligibility separately rather than assuming list visibility implies permission.

SetPortalAccess accepts AllowPortalAccess and four subflags, same defaults. It repeats the minor OR rule, updates all flags, replaces grant timestamp on every grant and clears revoked timestamp; revoke clears grant timestamp and sets revoked time. BUG-SEC-0006: explicit false cannot revoke a minor guardian link. Test birthday eve/on/after, leap-day DOB, unknown DOB, future/invalid-range stored DOB, omitted vs explicit false booleans and independently toggled permissions. No age transition job is established here; time alone does not rewrite previously stored link flags. Preserve historical-event expectations in audit tests rather than treating latest timestamp as a history log.

Unlink removes exactly the same-academy pair and returns204, or404 absent. No last-guardian/minor/primary protection or fallback promotion is implemented. Define required business policy before enforcing a minimum; removal must immediately deny that link's child reads while preserving access to other linked children. Unique index covers AcademyId+StudentId+GuardianId; no unique-primary constraint. Test concurrent duplicate link and two simultaneous primary links against real SQL, without claiming the race is reproduced. Foreign keys cascade on deleting Student/Guardian; that is separate from Unlink which removes neither person.
