# Phase 2B — Marketing-template consent

2026-10-01. Bounded local repair of [BUG-SEC-0010](../ISSUES/BUG-SEC-0010.md), using the accepted Astra finding. No repeat portal audit. Issue and Phase 2B remain **OPEN**. Sol High remains suitable.

## Policy and implementation

- Email/WhatsApp messages using a Marketing-category template require both the applicable channel consent and MarketingAllowed=true for the exact academy/recipient/type. Missing/foreign/other-type preferences fail closed through the existing channel-consent gate. Channel-consent failure retains precedence and its existing reason. Marketing opt-out yields BlockedConsent with “No recorded marketing consent for this recipient.” before connection readiness is considered.
- Utility, Authentication and Transactional retain existing channel-consent/connection behavior. Manual messages have no category contract; this repair does not classify their content as marketing. InApp and cancellation remain unchanged. Local Draft/Approved status and secure fixture flags do not prove provider approval or delivery.
- Create and external-channel Queue/RetryRequested share the current-preference check. Retry/queue recheck current scoped active template/category and reject unavailable/Disabled templates. A committed marketing or channel opt-out returns400 without changing notification fields or creating a route audit; cancellation remains permitted. Accepted retries keep the prior status/failure clearing, attempts, scheduling, delivery fields and actor-audit contract.
- Supported external channels are recognized case-insensitively after trimming and canonicalized at creation, including legacy template channel case. Case/whitespace Marketing categories are recognized. This prevents those variants from skipping the existing external consent branch. Unrecognized transports/categories and general notification status-transition policy are not redesigned.
- Only NotificationsController changed in product code. No frontend/DTO/schema/migration/permission/global-filter/provider changes. Existing preference upsert/timestamps and compose behavior are untouched.
