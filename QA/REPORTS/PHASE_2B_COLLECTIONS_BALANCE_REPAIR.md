## Change and preserved boundaries

[FinanceGovernanceController](../../apps/api/Controllers/FinanceGovernanceController.cs) now projects original `totalAmount`, applied `adjustedAmount`, non-Voided `paidAmount` and computed `balance = max(0, total - adjusted - paid)`. Completed and Reconciled count as paid; Voided does not. Payment aggregation is scoped to the invoice and academy. SQL performs the projection in one query, without per-invoice payment requests; the computed balance filters materialized overdue rows. Existing academy/status/due-date eligibility remains: own academy, not Paid/Cancelled, due strictly before UTC today. Deterministic due/ID ordering. Zero/negative-net rows are omitted, and summary `overdueInvoices` uses the same queue eligibility.

Follow-up creation rereads the current balance and rejects zero balance with 409 even if the invoice status is stale Issued. Paid/Cancelled rejection and foreign/missing 404 remain. Rejection creates neither work item nor audit; successful creation retains existing transactional audit behavior. It does not reconcile payments or change invoice status. Existing permissions, module mapping, generic task restrictions, migrations and authentication are unchanged.

[Finance workspace](../../apps/web/src/app/finance/page.tsx) and [Governance](../../apps/web/src/app/finance-governance/page.tsx) display `balance` with an explicit **Remaining due** label, including the selected follow-up context. Currency-aware default fraction digits preserve INR/USD cents instead of rounding ₹0.01 to zero. Original totals remain separately named in the API; no misleading replacement of `totalAmount` or gross fallback. Existing layout/navigation and refresh-aware success feedback are preserved.

Summary financial totals/FX grouping remain unchanged (BUG-DATA-0016). No restoration/unknown-payment-state/future-follow-up/assignee/duplicate-submit/concurrent-settlement business policy is introduced. This check does not certify a lock against simultaneous payment and follow-up creation.
