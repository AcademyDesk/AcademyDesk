Finance Governance GET/PUT and its FinanceControls requirement remain unchanged; this does not enable settings management for a Finance-only subscription.

Loading still requires all supported prerequisites to succeed; denied/network/server errors are not silently treated as default settings. Existing preview/theme/logo/signature/tax label/contact/print handlers remain otherwise unchanged. Invoice creation, amounts, tax arithmetic, fee-plan/student lookup, success handling, navigation and layout are not redesigned.
