# Phase 2B — Assessment grade consistency

2026-10-01. Sol High task allocation. Existing [BUG-DATA-0033](../ISSUES/BUG-DATA-0033.md) evidence reused. This repair is local and the issue and Phase2B remain OPEN.

## Result

[Shared grading policy](../../apps/api/Infrastructure/AssessmentGrading.cs) is used by the [Admin result writer](../../apps/api/Controllers/AssessmentsController.cs) and [Teacher result writer](../../apps/api/Controllers/TeacherPortalController.cs). Explicit automatic mode ignores the loaded grade and recalculates Pass/Fail from the linked scheme threshold, including the exact threshold. Explicit manual mode trims/preserves an intentional grade. Scores outside the range or beyond two decimal places are rejected; otherwise SQL rounding could store a passing score with a failing grade. Missing/foreign scheme and zero maximum score return400 with guidance rather than an exception. No scheme means automatic mode stores the score without inventing a grade; existing bands policy remains Pass/Fail.

The nullable `IsGradeManual` column records the distinction. The [migration](../../apps/api/Migrations/20261001113622_TrackAssessmentGradeMode.cs) adds only this nullable column, without a default, backfill or grade rewrite. Existing provenance remains unknown. The [Admin form](../../apps/web/src/app/assessments/page.tsx) preserves those existing grades and offers Automatic/Manual override. New entries default to Automatic; switching to Automatic recalculates on save. Older clients that omit mode retain their explicit nonblank-grade contract; null/empty/whitespace means automatic. Unknown historical grades cannot safely be retrospectively classified.

The form sends explicit mode, disables manual input in Automatic, accepts zero score and blocks blank/invalid input. Fresh persisted-result keys reset row editors after confirmed changes and assessment switches. Save success remains visible after refresh; failed readback is distinguished from a failed/unconfirmed write, and server guidance is shown without clearing entries. Existing permissions, enrollment admission and publication flows are unchanged by this repair; their separate registered issues remain open.
